## Successes

**S1. Multi-pass Codex review caught a story spec written against a false premise.** Story 4-7 was authored thinking `BPMAnalyzer.computeMelOnsetEnvelopeWithSubBands` was "energy-based onset detection." Codex thread `019e36de` surfaced that the function actually implements log-mel spectral flux verbatim — the original Story 4-7 would have been a no-op duplicate. Party-mode unanimously redirected to SuperFlux (Böck & Widmer 2013 frequency-axis max-filter extension). Without that review pass, we would have shipped 700+ lines of architecturally-equivalent code branded as a "new feature."

**S2. The HALT discipline grew teeth this epic.** Seven HALT events fired across the seven stories. Story 4-1 HALT-ed when `Double.bitPattern` snapshots were impossibly strict (relaxed to `%.1f`, with construction-level diff-emptiness as the byte-equality proof). Story 4-6 fired Branch C unambiguously after the threshold sweep collapsed controls from 4/4 to 0/4. Story 4-7 fired Branch B. **At every HALT, the dev agent surfaced concrete options rather than silently relaxing.** This is the most valuable cultural artifact this epic adds.

**S3. Per-track impact reports surfaced honest negative outcomes.** Three impact reports landed this epic: `4-5-bnns-impact-report.json` (`ml_acc1=0/82` — the 100%-abstain alarm), `4-6` BNNS diagnostic threshold sweep (`wrong_non_abstain_count=54/82` at forced-accept, `softmax_max_p95=0.294` bimodal at 125/175 BPM), `4-7-super-flux-impact-report.json` (`changedFinalBPM=82/82`, `namedDnBImproved=0/4`, `controlsPreserved=2/4`). **S4. The bundle-pull decision (Branch C) was the right call.** Codex's framing — "pre-1.0 is exactly when to be strict about not shipping an ineffective bundled binary" — closed the question. **S5. **S6. **S7. Consumer convert tool (`tools/coreml-convert/`) is the one genuinely shippable Epic 4 deliverable.** It survived Branch C unchanged. It's the only Python tooling that lands on main (DD #13 exception). pytest suite 3/3 passing. It's the BYOW on-ramp.

**S8. ## Challenges

**C1. The headline accuracy promise did not deliver. Epic 4 net OA300 Acc1 delta = 0 tracks; net GiantSteps Acc1 delta = 0 tracks.** Epic 3's retro identified ML as "the only remaining lever to grow OA300 Acc1 beyond 58/82 at default intensity." That lever produced exactly zero movement. The 4 named DnB triplet failures (Charly, Faraday_Bunker, Yin Yang, HEFT_Anagram 6) are still unresolved — they were T2 in Epic 3 retro's action items, became Story 4.5's named regression targets with quantified gates, hit 0/4 resolved, drove the Branch C bundle pull, and survive into post-Epic-4 with no remaining named story.

**C2. Story 4-4b shipped a model the team already knew would fail Story 4-5's HALT (b).** Per DD #11 option (i): "accept reference model with anticipated Story 4-5 HALT (b) — Story 4-5 dev addresses via re-train OR consumer override path at that time." The retrain didn't happen — Story 4-5 redirected to BYOW pivot, and Story 4-6 pulled the bundle entirely. In retrospect, the "ship and let next story handle it" call cost us two stories of momentum on a model that was destined for the bin.

**C3. The corpus quality ceiling was identified mid-epic, not pre-epic.** GiantSteps train+val data is 96 kbps MP3 (lossy). Filed 2026-05-09 as deferred-work after Story 4-4b training was complete. The Epic 3 retro flagged "onset-envelope quality on heavily-mastered material is the upstream weakness" — but that observation became a Story 4.5 dev-note rather than a pre-training *data* audit. The data ceiling was downstream of training, not upstream of it.

**C4. Story 4-5 review found a 100%-abstain failure mode that took until Story 4-6 to diagnose.** The shipped 4-5 impact report had `ml_acc1=0/82` and the team couldn't yet distinguish among 5 possible failure modes (ML never ran / features empty / inference failed / decoded out of range / decoded plausible but two-gate rejected). Story 4-6 added the diagnostic instrumentation to disambiguate. **The fact that diagnostic instrumentation arrived after the fact-of-needing-it means our trace surface was under-built for ML provenance.** Same pattern as Story 3-3b adding typed-evidence structs after the `[String: Any]` defect surfaced — pattern-match-and-fix, not anticipate-and-prevent.

**C5. SuperFlux (Story 4-7) regressed two DSP-correct controls.** Hellacopta + Darkgray Heart each lost ~0.5-1 BPM. The Branch B inert-ship close-out is honest about this — the case ships but joins no production preset — but consumers selecting `TechniqueSet.full` still get the regressing variant via `Set(allCases)` (deferred-work W1).

**C6. Test count growth + corpus benchmark wall-clock have not been monitored as a budget.** Epic 4 added ~125 tests. Many were architectural-invariant tests (256-combo ablation, parameterized smoke-lane gate, `MLDiagnosticTechnique` path coverage). They're load-bearing — but unit-test runs are slower than Epic 3 close-out. No story explicitly tracked the time delta.

**C7. `_bmad-output/perf-baselines/` accumulated 42 files; story-by-story baselines from Stories 4-3b and 4-4 are still there with no cleanup strategy.** Per-story baselines were valuable mid-Epic-4 for diff-against-prior-story; they're noise post-epic. Story-specific impact-report JSONs stay (diagnostic artifacts), but the perf-baseline trace bundles can probably be pruned.

**C8. The "spec-rescope mid-flight" pattern fired twice.** Story 4-7 rescoped from energy-based onset → SuperFlux. Story 4-5 rescoped mid-implementation to "BNNS infrastructure delivered; ML impact validation deferred to Story 4-6." Both rescopes were correct calls. But both cost wall-clock; both turned spec docs into accumulated-revision artifacts (Story 4-7 spec grew from ~468 lines → ~700 lines, then to 840 with close-out additions).

## Key Insights

1. **The bundled-model bet did not pay off, and pre-1.0 we correctly chose to delete the bet rather than preserve it.** Epic 4 originally bundled `giantsteps_v1.mlmodelc` (1254 KB FP32). It ships nothing today. The infrastructure to load any `.mlmodelc` ships. The convert tool ships. The DocC + MODEL_CARD + README + CLAUDE.md all frame as BYOW. That's a cleaner outcome than shipping a 2/82 model we don't believe in.

2. **DSP single-window Acc1 ceiling on OA300 is 55/82 — STILL.** The 256-combo ablation matrix (grown from 128 by `.superFluxOnset`) added one more case and did not break the ceiling. Per Epic 3 retro: "Pure technique-tuning in the existing N-case `DSPTechnique` family is at saturation." Epic 4 confirmed this with empirical evidence from the SuperFlux variant.

3. **HALT discipline is now the project's load-bearing safety net.** Seven HALT events fired across Epic 4. Each surfaced the issue to the Project Lead with concrete options. None were silently relaxed. Multi-pass pre-implementation review + HALT-at-execution-time is the regression-protection backbone for accuracy claims.

4. **Per-track impact reports are now non-negotiable for accuracy-affecting work.** Epic 3 retro made this a team agreement; Epic 4 confirmed its value at scale. The 100%-abstain alarm, the threshold-sweep collapse from 4/4 → 0/4 controls, and the SuperFlux 2-control-regression were all surfaced by impact reports — not by general benchmarks. Without the per-track artifact, the 100%-abstain would have shipped silently as "no regression."

5. **Diagnostic instrumentation as a feature should be designed BEFORE the technique that needs it, not after.** Story 4-6 added `MLDiagnosticSnapshot` because Story 4-5 left us blind. The pattern in Epic 3 (typed-evidence structs added after `[String: Any]` defects surfaced) is the same. Epic 5 / future epics should ask: "what diagnostic state must the trace carry for this new technique to be debuggable AT FAILURE, not after?" *Before* shipping the technique.

6. **The `.full` preset auto-includes via `Set(allCases)` is now a known footgun.** Story 4-7 W1 documents it. Consumers selecting `TechniqueSet.full` get `.superFluxOnset` despite the Branch B inert-ship. The invariant `TechniqueSet.full == Set(DSPTechnique.allCases)` is locked by tests, but the *semantics* of `.full` have not been re-litigated since it meant "all proven-helpful techniques." Epic 5 demo app + public API docs (Story 5-5) may force this conversation.

7. **What Epic 4 actually shipped was infrastructure, not accuracy.** Eight new public `Sendable` types: `MLEvaluation`, `EnsembleDecision`, `MLDiagnosticSnapshot`, `MLFeatureFrames`, `MLDiagnosticTechnique` (capability protocol), `BNNSTechnique`, `MLTechniqueError`, `EnsemblePolicy`. Plus `MLTechnique` protocol freeze, BYOW posture documented everywhere, consumer convert tool. The honesty of the close-out is that *accuracy* is not the deliverable — *consumer extensibility* is.
