## What fell short — stated plainly

**The beat-grid accuracy targets were measured and missed, and the automated refinement that followed did not close the gap.**

| Metric | Epic target | Measured (8.7, Rekordbox JAMS, 1264 tracks / 910 constant-tempo, ±70 ms) | Committed floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beat-position F (octave-norm, constant-tempo) | 0.75 | **0.37** | 0.33 (regression net) |
| Downbeat correctness-when-fired | 0.65 | **0.14** @ 4.3% fire | 0.10 |
| FR-29 P95 last-beat drift | 30 ms | **1652 ms** | 2.0 s (ceiling) |

The follow-up arc (8.9–8.12, operator's "3 pillars" of 2026-06-23) is an honest negative-to-pivot:

- **8.9** (one-shot DP tempo/phase refit) **regressed F 0.37→0.33 below the floor with drift unmoved, and was reverted** to byte-identical HEAD. Root cause: 8.4 had deliberately dropped the noisier DP re-measurement in favor of the clean BPM-stage tempo; the refit re-introduced that noise. Kept the decomposition spike + stratified drift reporting as the deliverable; shipped the `BeatGridTempoLock.bpm` manual primitive.
- **8.10** (continuous onset-comb refinement, `refineBeatGridTempo`, opt-in, default OFF) — fires **2/14** on the DAW-verified subset with a proven zero-regression monotonic reject-guard; where it fires it is a ~25× per-track drift collapse (0.10 s→0.004 s), but too sparse to move the corpus.
- **8.11** (drop-anchored downbeat: `DownbeatStrategy` `.metricalAccent`/`.structuralDrop`/`.combined`) and **8.12** (manual anchor reposition) **pivoted to Rekordbox-style hand-correction levers** rather than chasing auto-accuracy.

## Significant discovery — the beat-grid pivot (shapes Epic 9)

On real breakbeat/DnB material (the corpus is ~66% DnB/jungle/breaks, ~64% half-time-folded), the constant-tempo DP tracker cannot hit the aspirational grid-accuracy targets, and the accepted response was to **ship human override primitives (BPM-lock, downbeat-strategy selection, manual anchor reposition), not auto-precision.**

**Implication for Epic 9 (demo shell + ensemble picker):** the beat-grid UX should be built around **manual grid editing** (set-the-BPM + set-the-downbeat, the Rekordbox model), not trust in auto-detection or downbeat quantize. Concretely:
- Bar-snap / downbeat quantize is aspirational, not ready (4.3% fire, 0.14 correctness). Do not bet a demo feature on it.
- Octave-aware comparison is required, not transparent — ~64% of real material is half-time-folded; consumers matching BPM/grid must go through `TempoAgreement.octaveEquivalent(factor:)`.
- Beat-only quantize (1/4, 1/8) is the safe demo lane; bar/half-bar quantize is a stretch pending downbeat maturity.

## Decisions taken in this retrospective

1. **Epic-8 close posture: ACCEPT AS SHIPPED.** The auto-accuracy targets (0.75/0.65/30 ms) are documented aspirations with the committed regression floors (0.33/0.10/2.0 s) as nets; the manual hand-correction levers (8.11/8.12) + opt-in refinement (8.10) are the accepted mitigation. Epic 8 closes `done`. The aspirational targets remain in `epics.md` as the north star for a future beat-tracker story (not scheduled).

2. **Story 8.11 operator benchmark: done and recorded.** The `make benchmark-beatgrid` per-strategy run happened; measured `.combined` vs `.metricalAccent` numbers + the raised safety/usefulness floors are committed. No held-open condition from 8.11. Epic 8 closes clean (unlike Epic 7's held-open operator-run gate).

3. **`refineBeatGridTempo` (8.10) default: stays OFF; flip decision explicitly DEFERRED to documented future work** (operator + Codex consult, thread `019f1f0c`). Rationale: 2/14 changed on a 14-track subset is too narrow to change default behavior; both firing tracks share one failure mode (true-160 tracked half-time ~80), so it reads as a targeted correction, not a general default. Pre-1.0 freedom lowers the cost of flipping *later*, not the evidentiary bar for flipping *now*. See action item **AI-1** for the concrete flip gate.

## Action items

**AI-1 (documented deferral — `refineBeatGridTempo` default flip).** Keep `refineBeatGridTempo: Bool = false`, documented as an experimental/advanced precision refinement for stable long-span (`.window`/`.fullTrack`) grids. The flip to default-ON is deferred; recorded flip criteria (Codex thread `019f1f0c`), ALL required before flipping:
- Corpus: ≥ 75–100 scored DAW-verified tracks across genres/tempos/feels/density, incl. live-drifted and grid-stable electronic.
- Zero regression: `worsenedTempoError == 0` (or statistically negligible with a bounded max harm) across the full scored set.
- Meaningful activation: ≥ 10–20% `changedTempo` rate.
- Net benefit: `improvedTempoError > worsenedTempoError`, `meanDriftOn <= meanDriftOff`, P95/P99 drift not worse.
- Max-harm bound: no individual track's predicted final-beat drift worsens beyond ~10–20 ms.
- Coverage-split reporting (`.analysisWindow` / `.window` / `.fullTrack`) + a default-path latency budget (< 5% median for `.analysisWindow`, fixed upper bound for long tracks).
- At least one firing case outside the "half-time 160 tracked as 80" pattern.
- Codex suggested alternative: a `.fullTrack`-only preset (e.g. `longFormPrecision`) rather than a raw default flip. **Owner:** operator, at a future beat-grid precision story or an Epic 9 demo-evidence pass.
