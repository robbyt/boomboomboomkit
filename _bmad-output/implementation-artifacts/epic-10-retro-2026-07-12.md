## What went well

1. **Adversarial review earned its keep on the data-layer stories.** 10-1: Codex flagged a
   genuine lost-update race — the original `struct` + `@unchecked Sendable` did an
   unsynchronized compound read-modify-write of the bookmark map, so the `Sendable` contract
   lied. Hardened to a `Mutex`-guarded `final class` with a static lock and a
   snapshot-classify-**compare-and-swap**-merge (codec I/O outside the lock; no deadlock, no
   stale-drop). 10-2: register-first transactional order + `AddError.persistFailed` closed a
   ghost-row-on-`store`-throw bug. Load-bearing bugs, not nitpicks.
2. **Epic 9 AI-4 (factual-claims-grep + recorded decisions) held strongly.** Pre-spec greps
   caught **two epic-AC errors each** in 10-3 (grid on `CombinedAnalysisResult` not
   `AudioAnalysisResult`; no "partial" downbeat state) and 10-4 (no result carries LUFS; true-peak
   never nil), plus the "keep the existing Epic-8 `BeatGridView`, don't reinvent" catch (10-3 DD3).
   Recorded-decision discipline consistent across every story.
3. **MainActor-default-isolation friction was pre-empted**, per the Epic 9 carry-forward PSI —
   `nonisolated` on pure derivation, `@MainActor` on pure-value tests, no `KeyPathComparator` in
   `@State` defaults. It did not cost compile-red churn the way it did in Epic 9.
4. **Byte-identity discipline is airtight.** A five-story demo epic never once risked the library;
   `git diff Sources/ Tests/` was empty at every checkpoint.
5. **Epic 9 AI-3 landed cleanly** — the grandfathered primary-view "Load Model…" relocated into
   the registry-backed picker sheet (10-2 DD10/AC6), restoring FR-43.

## What was bumpy (systems, no blame)

1. **Three of five UI stories were spec-correct + review-clean and still failed live-GUI test.**
   This is the epic's headline lesson: for a demo-UI story, spec correctness and a clean 3-layer
   adversarial review are **necessary but not sufficient** — the UX intent only survives contact
   with the running app. 10-3 shipped a waveform-free timeline with nothing to audition against, a
   near-invisible transport, and no click target. 10-4 shipped a space-hungry single-number panel
   when the goal was an ebu-plot-style loudness-over-time graph. 10-5 shipped a redundant "?" affordance
   the demo already covered three other ways.
2. **Two reworks landed post-`done` with no governance artifact and contradicted their own
   shipped ACs + PRD-level FRs** before correct-course reconciled them. 10-3's rework contradicted
   AC5/AC6 + FR-39 + the PRD "no waveform" Non-Goal; 10-4's contradicted its scalar-panel ACs +
   FR-40. Both were caught by party-mode and cleaned up via `/bmad-correct-course` (two proposals:
   `sprint-change-proposal-2026-07-11.md`, `-story-10-4.md`) — AC retirement/inversion, FR
   amendments, architecture + epics reconciliation. The machinery worked, but the reworks
   should not have reached `done` contradicting themselves in the first place.
3. **Linux-authored / operator-run gauntlet split.** The reworks were authored in a container
   (`demo-lint` only); `demo-build`/`demo-test` + the GUI smoke are operator-run on Mac and, per
   the 10-3 change log, remain an **open trust gate** on the merged reworks.
4. **10-5 ran a full clean pipeline that was then thrown away** — but correctly. Live testing
   proved the story was prematurely sequenced ahead of Epic 11's authored prose; the mechanism +
   the resolver-seam pattern were preserved in `fr42-demo-popover-design-note.md` and re-homed as
   Story 11.6. Course-correction, not lost work.

with a design note, so Epic 11's plan already accounts for it — no epic-update-required, only the
