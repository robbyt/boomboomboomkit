## Intent

**Problem:** The three stacked PRs (198 Phases 1 to 4, 199 Story 15.6, 200 Story 15.7) carry 29 review findings from the operator and Codex. Three are blocking: rekordbox disassembly and decompilation sit in a public repo; `WaveformAnalyzer.analyze` traps on valid 8 kHz input; the Story 15.6 selector picks `.half` at every tempo whose period is not an integer number of hops, so the Story 15.7 fit returns nil at 124 to 174 BPM. The rest are reserved public API that does nothing, decoder drift and duplicate frames, a fit that fabricates tempo or accepts zero-support beats, and story specs whose ACs claim work that did not land.

**Approach:** Fix each finding on the branch that owns the file, bottom of the stack first, with regression tests at tempos that are not 120 BPM. Rewrite the Story 15.6 and 15.7 ACs to what landed and move struck ACs to explicit deferred items. Remove `docs/rekordbox-analysis/evidence/{native,hopper,models}` from the branch history, keep the files on disk gitignored in place, and split the Swift 6 toolchain fix into its own PR against develop.

**Decisions recorded at planning (reviewer offered alternatives; these are the picks):**

1. `WaveformChannelPolicy`, `WaveformOptions.channelPolicy`, and `WaveformOptions.blockSize` are deleted, not kept as `.mono`-only. Story 15.4b adds the enum when the cases work. `isCancelled` and `onProgress` become real: checked and reported per 256-bin chunk inside the per-bin loops, with a cancellation check between the three filter passes.
2. `WaveformAnalyzer.analyze` throws (does not clamp) when `midHighCutoffHz >= sampleRate / 2`, when `lowMidCutoffHz <= 0` or `lowMidCutoffHz >= midHighCutoffHz`, or when `columnsPerSecond` is outside `1...Int(sampleRate)`. Typed cases on `WaveformAnalyzerError`.
3. The evidence directories stay on disk at their current paths and are gitignored there (`docs/rekordbox-analysis/.gitignore`), so `FINDINGS.md` citations and `manifest.json` paths stay valid for anyone holding the local files. The README states which rows are local-only.
4. Story 15.6 selector scores each level by on-beat minus off-beat contrast averaged over every predicted position, including positions whose window held no peak. The decoder re-anchors each prediction on the frame it picked, uses non-overlapping windows (on-window of plus or minus a quarter period around the prediction; the off-window is the gap), emits no beat for a window whose maximum is zero, carries a `gridIndex` per beat so index gaps survive silence, and picks the downbeat phase in `0...3` with the largest summed downbeat support.
5. Story 15.7 fit seeds from `candidate.tempoBPM` and the median of `t_i - gridIndex_i * period`, requires at least 8 kept beats, abstains when the refit tempo leaves plus or minus 5 percent of the candidate tempo or when kept support is below 0.1, and its regression returns an optional instead of fabricating 120 BPM. Identifier becomes `joint-tempo-phase.v1.sub-frame-trimmed`; the "Huber" name is dropped everywhere (the loss is hard trimming).
6. Story 15.7 AC1 and AC5 are rewritten to the measured residual (constant one-hop-early anchor, about minus 10.9 ms on single-impulse clicks). AC3 (two-stream comparison) is struck; the twelve-fixture comb-versus-fit comparison moves to Story 15.9. Story 15.6 AC3, AC4, AC6, AC7 are rewritten so nothing claims trace fields, service wiring, or a benchmark run that did not happen. The PR 200 title and commit subject drop "kick-transient alignment".
7. Commits: PR 198 gets one new commit per touched story (15.2/15.3 test cleanup plus residual note; 15.4 option validation; preservation manifest and gitignore). PR 199 and PR 200 amend their single story commit; the operator's `49fb6e9` commit stays on top of 200 untouched.
8. The toolchain fix `e6a86ad` is cherry-picked onto a new branch from `origin/develop` and opened as its own PR; it stays in the stack until that PR lands and rebases away.
9. **Operator decision 2026-10-18:** no history rewrite and no force-push. The PRs land by squash, so the squash commit on develop carries only the final tree. Structural decoder correction (C). The guidance at `../rekordboxReader/docs/boomboomboomkit-beat-strength-and-ml-guidance.md` established that rekordbox's 0.05 threshold trims the activity span rather than accepting individual beats, and that phase is carried by a decoded state sequence rather than by resetting a clock at any positive maximum. The decoder now makes three separate decisions per predicted position: prediction (a clock that advances one period), observation (a local evidence rule: prominence over the median of a four-period background, at least three times the MAD with a 0.01 floor, searched first in a 0.15-period correction tier and then in the rest of the on-window), and correction (only a correction-tier observation moves the clock; an outer-tier observation after a prediction-only position re-anchors provisionally and is kept only if the next position confirms it within 0.05 period). A stream with no passing frame yields a no-evidence candidate with no beats, so the selector keeps its quarter baseline. Two limits are stated and pinned by tests: a beat train quieter than about 3 percent of the span maximum is not observed (`decoderQuietTrainLimitUnderOneTransient` at 0.02), and two aligned transients one period apart can confirm a wrong reacquisition (`decoderConfirmsWrongReacquisitionOnTwoAlignedTransients`; on that fixture the fit trims both noise beats and returns 120.000 BPM with kept fraction 9/11).

## Design Notes

Selector score per level: `mean_k(onSupport_k - offSupport_k)` over every predicted position `k`. `onSupport_k` is the max beat probability in `[pred_k - P/4, pred_k + P/4)`; `offSupport_k` is the max in `[pred_k + P/4, pred_k + 3P/4)`. Empty on-windows count as 0 and emit no beat; the prediction then free-wheels by `P` without re-anchoring. First prediction: the argmax of beat probability within the first full period of usable frames; if that window is all zero, advance by `P` and retry. Worked values on a clean 128 BPM click: quarter 1.0, half 0.0, double 0.0. On 120 BPM peaks with seed 60: quarter 0.0, half 0.0, double 1.0.

Fit: `phase_seed = median(t_i - g_i * P)` with `g_i = gridIndex`, `P = 60 / candidate.tempoBPM`. Trim at `0.15 * P` against the seed, refit by least squares on `(g_i, t_i)`, repeat up to three passes. Abstain (nil) when kept count is below 8, `keptFraction < 0.5`, mean kept support below 0.1, `abs(tempo / candidate.tempoBPM - 1) > 0.05`, or (Pass 3) the beats within `0.15 * P` of the returned line differ from the kept set it was fitted on.
