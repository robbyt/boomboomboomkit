## Story

As a **library maintainer**,
I want **beat-grid accuracy validated against the OA300 corpus (DAW-oracle ground truth) plus a stratified DnB subset — F-measure floor and beat-position tolerance committed as numeric ACs, AND a real-audio validation of the Story-8.5a downbeat detector (fire rate, correctness-when-fired, abstain rate)**,
so that **Epic 8 cannot close without committed beat-grid accuracy gates equivalent to the FR-10 BPM accuracy floors, future regressions surface immediately, and the opt-in downbeat feature that reaches a live DJ deck stops being "trust me, it's conservative" and becomes a measured number.**

## Dev Notes

### Why this is the epic-closing story

Story 8.4 shipped a beat-tracker, 8.5 a consistency contract, 8.5a a downbeat detector — three new beat-grid behaviors with **no accuracy floor guarding any of them** (the same anti-pattern as a public type with no producer). This story is the regression net: it commits numeric F-measure + drift + downbeat gates equivalent to the FR-10 BPM-accuracy floors, so the epic cannot close on unproven beat-grid surface. It is also where the opt-in downbeat feature — which reaches a live DJ deck when a consumer flips `detectDownbeats = true` — gets a measured fire-rate/correctness number instead of the design-intention word "conservative".

### Sequencing vs Story 8.8 (operator decision 2026-06-14)

8.7 and 8.8 stay **separate**; 8.7 comes first. 8.7 **owns the shared JAMS plumbing** (the `JAMSDecoder.swift` + the `jams`/`mir_eval` pyproject deps); 8.8 (the one-time migration of `daw-oracle.json` / `oa300-ground-truth.json` / `4-dnb-triplet-targets.json` to JAMS) **reuses** that decoder later. Critically, 8.7 reads its **own** `daw-oracle-beats.json` (from `make oracle-generate-beats`), NOT 8.8's migrated `daw-oracle.json` — so 8.7 is self-contained and does not depend on 8.8 (verified in the pre-spec grep — DD-2). 8.8 is the natural next story after this one.
